# KovaGPT capability inventory

Audited **2026-09-05**, source checkpoint **`1eb7ef53`**. The authoritative progress baseline remains **23.2%**; this inventory does not recalculate it. It covers all 27 master-handoff areas, including Kova differentiators. Later packages are recorded explicitly below and must be checked against their eventual integrated commit.

This is a source and evidence inventory, not a deployment certificate. “Bounded source” means the described implementation exists under its stated limits; it does not mean every possible feature in the category is complete. “Pending package” and “Partial source” contain autonomous work that must not be relabeled as owner setup. Official comparison sources and their platform limits are in [the current surface audit](product-parity/chatgpt-surface-audit.md).

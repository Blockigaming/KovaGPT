## Applicability register

- **Web/PWA applicable:** all portable master-floor Chat, Work, Tasks, Projects, Library, Images, Memory, Study, account, developer, organization and static Sites workflows are tracked in the [27-area source inventory](../feature-parity.md). Missing portable source remains autonomous work.
- **Native platform N/A:** desktop GUI automation, device/browser history capture, appshots, local multi-folder workspaces, native browser extensions, Apple Messages, OS pets and dedicated CLI/IDE distribution. Kova has no corresponding native product; PWA behavior does not establish one.
- **Explicit exclusion:** voice, audio, dictation and recording. New reference releases do not override the owner's decision.
- **Proprietary/external:** identical model routing/ranking, private OpenAI APIs/hosting, partner Sign in with ChatGPT and private acceptance/rollout controls. Implement available open protocols with Kova identity instead of inventing access.
- **Specialized/regulated:** institutional Edu/Teachers deployments, medical/clinician products, trusted-access cyber/Daybreak, Rosalind and compliance/residency guarantees require their own provider access, policy and evidence. Ordinary Study, bounded analysis, organization roles and developer tools are limited equivalents, not certifications. Do not treat a vertical marketing name as authorization for a new regulated service.

## Evidence limits and repeatability

No authenticated OpenAI account, private enterprise console, paid plan, geographic restriction or device permission was inspected. Public document access does not complete visual parity. Before release, rerun the lawful applicable UI/state matrix on the exact Kova candidate: loading/error/offline/recovery, mobile/desktop and landscape, keyboard/touch/screen-reader semantics, zoom/reduced motion, light/dark, large content and actual authorized multi-account flows. WebKit simulation is not actual Safari hardware. Record immutable CI/review/deployment IDs; never transfer an older head's success to the new one.

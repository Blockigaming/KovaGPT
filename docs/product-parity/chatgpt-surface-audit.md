## Audit limitations and review cadence

The public-surface audit is incomplete because outbound retrieval was unavailable. An administrator must rerun it from an approved network and record screenshots or response hashes as audit evidence. Subscription, enterprise, education, country, mobile, and feature-flag variants remain unobserved. Findings must never be promoted to **already complete** without functional, authorization, tenant-isolation, responsive, accessibility, state, analytics, SEO, and automated-test evidence.

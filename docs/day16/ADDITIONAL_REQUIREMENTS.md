# KovaGPT Additional Day 16 Product Requirements

These requirements were explicitly added after the canonical Finalized Goal.
They extend the required product scope but do not modify or replace
`FINAL_GOAL.md`.

KovaGPT must also include:

- reliable, privacy-safe product analytics;
- referral and campaign attribution;
- excellent onboarding with measurable completion and skip behavior;
- truthful and well-timed upgrade prompts;
- payment failure and past-due recovery;
- cancellation feedback;
- feature-to-upgrade attribution;
- feature-to-subscription attribution;
- churn and retention measurement;
- real customer testimonials only when they are genuine and explicitly
  approved for publication.

Testimonials must never be fabricated, invented, strengthened beyond what the
customer actually said, or represented as customer evidence without explicit
approval.

Analytics must not contain prompts, assistant responses, files, email content,
connector content, secrets, tokens, passwords, or arbitrary private text.
